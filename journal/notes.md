# Trade Journal

Freeform notes on trades worth remembering - the "why" behind entries/exits
that the raw data can't capture. Newest entry on top.

Prompted especially by `data/processed/bad_trade_flags.csv` after a
`detect_bad_trades.py` run - when a trade gets flagged, write down what
actually happened here (thesis, what changed, what you'd do differently).
